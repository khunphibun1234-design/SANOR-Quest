HOW TO PLAY

Ready to explore?

Read the clue and find the object
that matches it.

  • On a phone: Hold an arrow button to move.
  • On a computer: use WASD or the arrow keys
  • Walk up to an object and select “Explore” or Space
  • Scroll up to zoom in and down to zoom out.
  • Hold the right mouse button and drag: Rotate through 360° and tilt the camera up–down
  • Rotate with ↶ ↷ or Q / E too
  • Buttons − / + zoom and ⤾ Reset the camera
  • Try again without losing points.
  • Tap the sound button to hear the word.
