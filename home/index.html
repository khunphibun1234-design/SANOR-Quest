    How to play

    01 · Explore on footUse WASD, the arrow keys or the on-screen buttons. Tap a place label or select “Take me there” to go there

    02 · TalkGo to your destination and select “Talk” or press Space. Choose a reply or put the words in order. You can try again.

    03 · Look around the houseScroll to zoom. Hold the right mouse button and drag to rotate 360°, or use Q / E and the on-screen buttons

    04 · Make a welcome cardWrite a welcome card with 3 sentences, practise speaking and reflect on your work.

    XP is a reward for completing activities. You mark your own speaking practice.
