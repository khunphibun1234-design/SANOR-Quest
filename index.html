      LET YOUR ADVENTURE BEGIN

      Ready to explore?

      1. Choose your world

        Tap a district on the map or a card below to see its story and missions.

      2. Choose a story

        Play Map 01 to welcome Alex into your home, or choose Map 02 to show Alex around school

      3. Walk and use English

        In Map 01 Use WASD, the movement buttons, or tap a place label. Scroll to zoom. Right-drag to rotate. On a phone, use the camera buttons.

      4. Keep your story in your passport

        Every world adds XP and stamps to one passport. Return in the same browser to continue.

      5. Choose your Thai help

        Starter shows Thai translations, Explorer hides them until you tap, Challenger is English only. Change it any time with the ภาษาไทย button or in your passport.
